# Québec Studio Mods

Plugins and packages for Craft CMS, Laravel and Statamic, made by
[Québec Studio](https://quebecstudio.com) in Rouyn-Noranda,
Abitibi-Témiscamingue, Quebec.

## Cookie Consent Kit

An opt-in cookie consent banner: no third-party cookie, script or embed loads
before the visitor agrees. Written for Quebec's Law 25 and usable under the
GDPR.

| Package | For |
|---|---|
| `quebecstudio-mods/craftcms-consent-kit` | Craft CMS 5 and 6 |
| `quebecstudio-mods/laravel-consent-kit` | Laravel 11 to 13 |
| `quebecstudio-mods/consent-kit-core` | the framework-free core both are built on |
